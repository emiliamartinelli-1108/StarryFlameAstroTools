StarryFlameAstroTools

Planetary science in your browser

Free astronomy and planetary science tools

StarryFlameAstroTools is a growing collection of free astronomy and planetary science tools that run entirely in your browser. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

Open a tool, change its settings and watch what happens. Every tool is a single web page, so it works on a desktop, a tablet or a phone.

Right now StarryFlameSPH, a planet collision simulator, is available. More tools are on the way.
